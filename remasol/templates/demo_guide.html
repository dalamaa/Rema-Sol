For the merchant

Less refund investigation. Fewer support handoffs.

RemaSol verifies the payment and calculates the excess from the finalized Solana transfer. Solana's low network fees make a small 0.003 SOL refund practical, while verifiable transaction records let merchants and customers inspect what happened. Eligible, verified demo payments can use self-service recovery; uncertain payments stay in manual review.

Without RemaSol

  • Customer contacts support.
  • Merchant investigates transaction proof.
  • Merchant calculates the excess.

With RemaSol

  • RemaSol verifies the destination, sender, amount, and finalization on Solana.
  • The excess comes from the verified payment, not customer-entered data.
  • The customer requests the eligible excess through Recovery.
  • The system keeps durable payment and refund records with transaction links.
