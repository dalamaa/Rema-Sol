The merchant problem

A simple payment mistake shouldn't become a support problem.

A customer accidentally sends more SOL than the price of your product. Now you have to verify the payment, work out the difference, confirm where the refund should go, and send it back. Meanwhile, the customer waits for a response. What should have been a simple purchase becomes extra work for both of you.

How it works

From payment mistake to a clear resolution.

01

Create a checkout

Set your price in SOL. RemaSol gives each order its own payment address.

02

Verify the payment

RemaSol checks the finalized transaction on Solana and calculates whether the customer paid the correct amount or sent too much.

03

Recover the excess

Eligible customers can request a refund through a guided recovery flow. If the destination cannot be verified safely, the case goes to manual review.
